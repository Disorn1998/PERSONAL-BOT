# Exam-week plan (fictional demo)

Self-authored sample plan for international students trying persona-bot clones.
Not medical or counseling advice — just a study-structure demo.

## Five-day outline

1. **Map** — list topics + mark weak / medium / strong.
2. **Blocks** — 50 minutes focus + 10 minutes break; 3–4 blocks/day.
3. **Active** — teach-back out loud or write a one-page summary without notes.
4. **Mix** — interleave two subjects instead of finishing one entirely.
5. **Light day** — shorter review, sleep priority, no new heavy topics.

## Quick checks

- Can you answer yesterday’s starter questions without peeking?
- Did you sleep at least 7 hours most nights this week?
- Is your phone in another room during focus blocks?

## When stuck

Narrow the goal: one worked example, one definition, one practice question —
then stop and schedule the next review.
