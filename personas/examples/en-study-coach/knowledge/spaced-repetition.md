# Spaced repetition (demo study notes)

These notes are **self-written demo content** for the `en-study-coach` example
pack. They are not a textbook reprint.

## Idea

Spaced repetition means reviewing material just before you would forget it.
Short, frequent reviews beat one long cram session for long-term recall.

## Simple schedule (demo)

| Review | When after first learn |
|---|---|
| R1 | same day (evening) |
| R2 | +1 day |
| R3 | +3 days |
| R4 | +1 week |
| R5 | +2 weeks |

If a card feels easy, skip ahead one step. If you miss it, move it back one step.

## Card tips

- One fact or one prompt per card.
- Prefer active recall (question on front) over rereading notes.
- Keep answers short so you can grade yourself honestly.
